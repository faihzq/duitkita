Privacy Policy

DuitKita (my.duitkita.app)

Last updated: 4 August 2026

DuitKita is a personal finance app for tracking shared group savings, bills, debts and trip planning. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

In short: your data is stored in Google Firebase under our project and is used only to run the app. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Who we are

DuitKita is operated by an independent developer. For any privacy question, correction or deletion request, contact duitkita.app@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Email address and display name — from email sign-up or Google Sign-In.
  • Username (a unique handle so others can add you to a group or trip).
  • Phone number and profile photo — optional, only if you add them.

Financial information you enter

  • Groups you create or join: names, monthly amounts, savings goals, members.
  • Bank details you choose to add to a group — bank name, account number and account holder name — so members know where to send payments.
  • Payments, expenses, loans and bills: amounts, dates, notes and status.
  • Payment receipts you upload (images or PDFs).

Trip information

  • Trips, destinations, dates and the travellers you add.
  • Itinerary stops: titles, times, notes, place names, Google Maps links and, where you provide them, map coordinates.
  • Tickets and documents you attach to a stop (images or PDFs).

Device and technical information

  • A push notification token, so we can send you reminders and activity alerts.
  • Crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) — device model, OS version and the error.
  • Basic usage analytics (via Firebase Analytics), such as which screens are opened.

Stored on your device only

  • Whether you have completed the intro screens.
  • Trip tickets you have opened, cached so they remain available offline.

4. Who can see your data

DuitKita is a shared app, so some information is visible to people you choose to share it with:

  • Group members can see your name, profile photo, and your payment amounts, dates, notes, receipts and payment status within that group.
  • Group admins can additionally approve or reject your payments and expenses, and can add or remove members.
  • Trip travellers can see the trip's itinerary, stops and any tickets attached to it.
  • Anyone signed in can look up a username to find the matching account when adding a member or traveller.

Nothing is public. Only signed-in members of a group or trip can read its contents.

5. Third-party services

We do not sell or rent your data. We use these providers to run the app:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, file storage, push notifications, crash reporting and analytics. See Firebase privacy.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google Maps Platform — when you ask the app to estimate a drive between two stops, those two locations are sent to Google. Opening a place or route hands off to the Google Maps app or website. See Google privacy.

6. Storage and security

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) and protected by access rules that restrict each record to the people entitled to see it — group members for group data, travellers for trip data, and you alone for your personal debts and profile. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.

No system is perfectly secure. Please avoid storing information in notes or attachments that you would not want other members of that group or trip to see.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

Deleting your account in the app

Open Profile → Delete account, confirm, and your account is removed immediately. No email or waiting period is involved.

What deletion removes

  • Your profile, username and photo
  • Your personal loans and bills
  • Your membership of every group and trip
  • Receipts and tickets you uploaded
  • Your sign-in credentials

What stays, and why

  • Payments you already made to a group, so the group's balance and other members' history stay correct. These keep only the name already visible to those members.
  • Groups and trips other people are still using — if you owned one, ownership passes to another member or traveller so it does not become unusable. A group or trip with nobody else on it is deleted with your account.

If you cannot access the app

Email duitkita.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will do the same thing on your behalf, normally within 30 days.

If you would like the retained payment records anonymised as well, say so and we will do it.

8. Children

DuitKita is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Your choices

  • Notifications can be turned off in your device settings at any time.
  • Photo and file access is only requested when you choose to attach a receipt or ticket.
  • You can edit or remove most information you have entered from within the app.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or its correction or deletion, by email.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app.
