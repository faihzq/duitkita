Delete your DuitKita account

DuitKita (my.duitkita.app)

Last updated: 4 August 2026

You can delete your DuitKita account and its data at any time, from inside the app. No email, approval or waiting period is required.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open DuitKita and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Read what will be removed, then type DELETE to confirm.
  5. Tap Delete account. If you signed up with an email address you may be asked for your password; if you signed in with Google you will be asked to confirm through Google.

Your account and data are removed straight away, and you are returned to the sign-in screen.

Delete some of your data without closing your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove individual records. Each of these can be deleted on its own, from inside the app:

DataSteps
A personal loan or bill, and its repayment records Open the Debts tab → tap the record → Delete → confirm
A payment you submitted, and the receipt attached to it Open the Groups tab → tap the group → payment history → tap your payment → Delete → confirm
An expense request you submitted, and its receipt — while it is still awaiting a decision Open the Groups tab → tap the group → tap the expense → Delete request → confirm
An itinerary stop, and any ticket attached to it Open Trips from the Home tab → tap the trip → tap the stop → Delete (trip organiser only)
Your name, phone number or profile photo Open the Profile tab → Edit profile → change or clear the field → save

Deleting a record removes it and its uploaded files straight away. To have anything else removed, email duitkita.app@gmail.com describing what you would like deleted; we normally action requests within 30 days.

If you cannot access the app

Email duitkita.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, using the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the same data on your behalf, normally within 30 days.

Groups and trips that nobody else is using are deleted along with your account. There is no additional retention period, and deleted accounts cannot be restored.

What is kept, and why

  • Payments you already made to a group remain in that group's records, so its balance and the other members' contribution history stay correct. These keep only the name that was already visible to members of that group.
  • Expense requests that have already been approved or rejected stay in the group's records for the same reason: an approved expense has been paid out of the group's money, so removing it would leave the balance wrong for everyone else. A request still awaiting a decision can be deleted by whoever submitted it.
  • Groups and trips that other people are still using are not deleted. If you created one, ownership passes to another member or traveller so the group or trip remains usable. If nobody else is on it, it is deleted with your account.

If you would also like the retained payment records anonymised, email duitkita.app@gmail.com and we will do it.
